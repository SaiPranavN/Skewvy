Skewvy favicon — carrot and black

The mark is a deliberately skewed W cut from a single bold black shape on a carrot-orange square. Its heavy silhouette and nearly square frame carry the neo-brutalist style at favicon sizes. There is no standalone S.

Contents: favicon.svg (preferred), favicon.ico (16/32/48px), favicon-16.png, favicon-32.png, apple-touch-icon.png (180px).

Next.js App Router: rename favicon.svg to icon.svg and place icon.svg, favicon.ico, and apple-touch-icon.png in the app/ directory. For other frameworks, place the files in public/ and add a link to /favicon.svg in the document head.
